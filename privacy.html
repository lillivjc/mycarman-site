MyCarMan Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

MyCarMan ("the App") is a car maintenance log for iPhone, published by Joe Lill ("we", "us"). This policy covers what the App stores, what it does not do, and the choices you have.

Short version: the App collects nothing. All of your data stays on your device, or in your own Apple iCloud.

Data stored by the App

Everything you enter — vehicles, service records, maintenance reminders, notes, and receipt or photo attachments — is stored first on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, that data also syncs through your own private CloudKit database provided by Apple's iCloud service, protected by your Apple ID. We do not operate, see, or have access to any copy of that data; Apple's storage of your iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Vehicle photos and receipts are stored in the App's on-device cache, which is covered by iOS sandboxing, and mirrored into your private iCloud database so they sync to your other devices signed into the same Apple ID.

Data we collect about you

None. The App contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party tracking, and no user accounts. The developer has no servers and no backend. The App communicates only with Apple services you already use: iCloud for sync, and the App Store for purchases and updates.

Notifications

The App can schedule local notifications to alert you when maintenance is due soon or overdue. These are generated on your device from the reminders you create in the App; we never send anything to you from a server. The permission is requested only after you create your first reminder, and can be refused or later revoked in iOS Settings at any time.

Device features used

  • Photo library: accessed exclusively through the iOS system photo picker; only the photos you explicitly select are read.
  • Documents: accessed exclusively through the iOS system document picker; only files you explicitly choose are opened.
  • Notifications: local only, opt-in as described above.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a vehicle (with its records and attachments) inside the App and the data is removed from your device and from iCloud.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.
  • To review or delete iCloud data at the account level, use iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, as described in Apple's documentation.

Important disclaimers

MyCarMan is a record-keeping and reminder tool. It is informational only and is not a professional vehicle inspection, diagnostic, estimating, or repair service. Maintenance intervals you enter, and any due dates the App computes from your own history, are your responsibility to verify against your vehicle manufacturer's guidance and your trusted service provider's recommendations. We are not liable for any vehicle condition, repair costs, or outcomes resulting from use of the App.

Children

MyCarMan is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13, nor does it knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy
