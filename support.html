Frequently Asked Questions

Does my data leave my phone? Privacy

  • All vehicles, service records, reminders, notes, and attachments are stored on your device.
  • If you're signed into iCloud, that data syncs through your own private iCloud database — the developer has no servers and never sees your data.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. See the full Privacy Policy.

How do I add a vehicle? Getting started

  • Tap + on the vehicle list. The catalog covers 40+ makes with common models.
  • Fill in year, make, model, owner, VIN, and odometer — only make and model are required.

How do service records work? Getting started

  • Open a vehicle → Add → record the date, category, cost, odometer, and shop.
  • Attach receipts or photos; they're stored with the record and included in exports.

How do maintenance reminders work? Reminders

  • Open a vehicle → Add Reminder. Set an interval in miles and/or months.
  • The reminder counts down from your most recent matching service record.
  • Notifications are local only — they're generated on your device, never sent from a server. Grant notification permission when asked, or later in iOS Settings.
  • Mileage-based reminders update when you add a record with a new odometer reading.

How do I export or print my history? Export

  • Open a vehicle → share button (⤴) → Export History…
  • Choose CSV (spreadsheet-friendly) or PDF (a printable report to hand to a buyer or shop).

Can I import history from another app? Import

  • Yes — export a CSV from your old app, then open a vehicle → share menu → Import from CSV…

How do I delete a vehicle? Data

  • Open the vehicle → Delete (trash icon) → confirm. This removes the vehicle, its records, and attachments from your device and iCloud.

Is it a subscription? Purchase

  • No — MyCarMan is a one-time purchase. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

What iPhones does it support? Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Data syncs across your iPhone and iPad with the same Apple ID.
